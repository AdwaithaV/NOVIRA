Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

NOVIRA Studio Content Automation is a personal application used exclusively by the owner of the NOVIRA STUDIO Pinterest account. The application connects to Pinterest to create, schedule, publish and analyse content on the owner's Pinterest boards.

Information accessed

When authorised, the application may access the authenticated account's profile information, boards, Pins and account-level performance information. It does not access other users' private Pinterest information.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate, monitor and troubleshoot the application and to manage content belonging to the authenticated account.

Sharing and selling

The application does not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Authentication credentials and API tokens are stored privately and used only to operate the application. Product information may be obtained through authorised affiliate or product APIs. Published content may contain clearly disclosed affiliate links.

Retention and control

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate, monitor and troubleshoot the application. Pinterest access can be revoked through the connected Pinterest account or developer settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact NOVIRA STUDIO through its Pinterest profile.
